# Roadmap: İkra Web

## Overview

İkra Web is the marketing/landing site for the İkra Quran verses & Sahih hadith widget mobile app, hosting the Privacy Policy / Terms / Support pages required for App Store and Google Play submission. Built with Next.js App Router + Tailwind on Vercel, English-only, mirroring the app's emerald/gold/navy identity.
