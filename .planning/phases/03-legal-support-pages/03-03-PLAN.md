      - 4-6 sentences across 1-2 paragraphs.

      - MUST state these materials are NOT licensed for redistribution, public broadcast, or commercial use outside the app.

      - MUST state the app is provided "as is" and "as available" without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement.

      - 2-3 sentences.

    Section 7 — Limitation of liability (`id="limitation-of-liability"`):
      - MUST state that to the maximum extent permitted by law, we are not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising from use of the app.
      - MUST state aggregate liability (if any) is limited to the amount paid by the user for the app in the 12 months preceding the claim.
      - 2-3 sentences.

      - MUST state these Terms are governed by `the laws of the State of Delaware, United States`, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.
      - MUST include a brief note suggesting that local consumer-protection laws may also apply where the user resides.
      - 2 sentences.

    Section 9 — Changes (`id="changes"`):
      - MUST state we may update these Terms and that the "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
      - MUST state that continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
      - 2 sentences.
